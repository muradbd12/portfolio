01 · About

Two years of keeping critical systems online.

I'm a detail-oriented Electrical & Electronic Engineering professional with hands-on experience across network infrastructure, data center maintenance, and Terminal Operating Systems (TOS). My work has centered on Chittagong Port Authority — configuring 10+ Cisco and BDCOM switches/routers, managing 10+ mesh wireless access points and 20+ CCTV cameras, and resolving complex integration issues that keep one of Bangladesh's busiest seaports running without interruption.

Before moving into ICT, I spent time in industrial automation — commissioning control systems for H2O2 and H2 plants, installing field instruments to P&ID specification, and routing DCS panel terminations. That background in precision, documentation, and systems-thinking now shapes how I approach every network I manage.
